FAQ

Everything about app development

⚖️
Should I build a native or cross-platform app?

It depends on the project. Cross-platform apps (one codebase for iOS and Android) are faster and cheaper to build and maintain, and ideal for most cases. Native development makes sense when you need maximum performance or deep access to specific device features.

🔌
Can the app connect to my current management system?

Yes. We build the app to connect via API to your ERP, CRM, database, or any system you already use, so information stays in sync at all times.

⏱️
How long does app development take?

A first functional version (MVP) is usually ready in 4 to 8 weeks. A full app with multiple features can take 2 to 4 months, depending on scope.

🏬
Do you handle publishing to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes, we handle the entire publishing process, including developer account setup, store policy compliance, and future updates.

➕
What if I need to add new features later?

The app is built on a modular architecture designed to grow, so you can add new screens, features or integrations without rebuilding the app from scratch.
