FAQ

Everything about web design & SEO

⏱️
How long does it take to get my website ready?

A landing page or institutional site is usually ready in 2 to 3 weeks. A more complex site, with multiple sections, a blog, or e-commerce, can take between 4 and 8 weeks depending on scope.

🔍
Will my site show up on Google from day one?

The site is published technically optimized for SEO from day one (speed, structure, metadata, structured data), but organic ranking in search engines is a process that takes weeks or months depending on competition in your industry.

✏️
Can I edit the site content myself afterwards?

Yes. Depending on the project, we deliver the site with a simple admin panel so you can update text, images and prices without relying on a developer for every change.

📱
Will the site look good on mobile phones?

Yes, all our sites are designed mobile-first: built first for phones and then adapted for tablet and desktop, since most traffic comes from mobile devices.

🛟
Do you include maintenance and support after delivery?

Yes, we offer maintenance plans that include updates, backups, security monitoring and content adjustments, plus support for any questions or issues that come up.
