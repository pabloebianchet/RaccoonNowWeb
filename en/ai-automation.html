What can you automate?

Any repetitive process that eats up your team's time can be automated. These are the most common ones we implement.

24/7 customer support

AI chatbots that answer questions, manage orders and hand off to humans when needed. Integrated with WhatsApp, email and your CRM.

📄

Document processing

Automatic data extraction from invoices, contracts, forms and emails. Classification, validation and loading into your systems with no manual work.

🔄

System syncing

Integration between CRM, ERP, e-commerce, accounting and any tool. Data flows automatically, with no manual copy-pasting.

📊

Automated reporting

Sales, operations and finance reports generated and sent automatically on the schedule you define. No Excel, no manual work.

Internal AI agents

AI assistants trained on your company's own knowledge. They answer your team's questions, draft documents and analyze data in seconds.

Marketing & follow-up

Automated email and WhatsApp sequences, lead follow-up, scoring and assignment to sales reps based on prospect behavior.
