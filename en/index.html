Do you work with companies in the US and internationally?

Yes. Raccoon Now works as a 100% remote team with companies across the United States, Canada, Europe and Latin America, adapting to each client's time zone, language and compliance requirements.

How much does custom software development cost?

Cost depends on scope, required integrations and project complexity. We work with fixed-price proposals per phase, so you know exactly what you'll receive and how much you'll invest before we start.

How long does a typical project take?

A professional website can be ready in 2 to 4 weeks. A management system or custom software platform usually takes between 6 and 12 weeks, depending on modules and integrations.
