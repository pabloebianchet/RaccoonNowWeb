FAQ

Everything about ERP & CRM management systems

⚖️
What's the difference between a custom ERP and an off-the-shelf one?

Commercial ERPs charge per-user licenses and usually include generic modules your business won't use. A custom ERP is built only with the modules you need, with no recurring license costs, and adapts to your processes instead of forcing you to change how you work.

📂
Can I migrate data from my current system?

Yes. As part of the project, we migrate your customer, product, inventory and transaction data from your current system (spreadsheets, another ERP, etc.) so you start without losing your history.

🏬
Can the system be used from multiple locations or devices?

Yes, the system is built as a web platform accessible from any device with an internet connection, allowing different branches or teams to work on the same data in real time.

📈
Does it include reports and analytics?

Yes, we include custom dashboards and reports on sales, inventory, customers and finance, so you can make decisions based on up-to-date business data.

📈
What if my company grows and I need more features?

The system is built on a modular architecture, so new modules (e-invoicing, e-commerce, logistics, etc.) can be added as your business needs them.
